# What is Orchestra Sync?

Imagine a world where the wall between Design and Development crumbles down. That is the world **Orchestra Sync** builds for you.

At its heart, **Orchestra Sync** is the conductor of your design system's symphony. It is a specialized Figma plugin designed to bridge the gap between your visual creativity and the rigid world of code.

## The Problem
In the traditional workflow, a designer meticulously crafts a "Primary Blue" in Figma. They hand it off to a developer. The developer manually types `#18A0FB` into their codebase. 
Weeks later, the design changes to `#0D8ADB`. The developer misses the memo. The product is now inconsistent. 

## The Solution
**Orchestra Sync** automates this specific handoff. It takes your Figma Variabes—your colors, your spacing, your typography—and instantly transforms them into **Design Tokens** (JSON code) that live directly in your engineering team's GitHub repository.

It doesn't just "export" a file. It acts as a live link. When you update a color in Figma, Orchestra Sync pushes that update directly to the code. It ensures that the "truth" in your design file is the exact same "truth" in the production app.

It is not just a tool; it is the single source of truth engine for your product's design language.

## 💼 LinkedIn (Professional & Detailed)

**Headline:** Bridge the gap between Figma and your Codebase with Orchestra Sync! 🚀

**Body:**

I'm excited to introduce **Orchestra CLI** — a new open-source tool designed to automate the handoff between Design and Development.

We all know the pain of manually updating hex codes, spacing values, and typography settings every time a design changes. It's tedious, error-prone, and keeps designers and developers out of sync.

**Orchestra Sync solves this by:**
✅ **Automating Design Tokens:** Sync variables directly from Figma to your GitHub repository.
✅ **Multi-Platform Support:** Ready-to-use tokens for Web (CSS/SCSS), Android (Kotlin), iOS (Swift), and Flutter (Dart).
✅ **Developer Experience:** Integrated CLI to auto-generate theme files in your build pipelines.

It acts as a single source of truth, ensuring your production app always matches your Figma designs.
